# Round 01: WolfReasoner training and evaluation contract

**Decision:** Identify the most reliable tournament entry with evidence from fair mixed-policy play. The approved [workplan](../../WORKPLAN.md) determines candidate order: repaired Rules baseline, LLM direct, and Belief tracking + LLM. Freeze each version before comparison; adding RL is an experiment gated by evidence, not a required deployment outcome.

**Rule and runtime checks.** Match the local [Werewolf rules](../../werewolf.md): seven seats (four villagers, one seer, two wolves), peaceful first night, private investigation results, votes hidden until tally, day vote ties eliminate nobody, wolf night target ties favor the lower target seat, wolves win at parity or after three consecutive days without elimination, and village wins after both wolves are eliminated. Check legal actions and per-seat information boundaries, including discussion visibility and timing. Repair the role-dependent vote filter and confirm authoritative terminal capture for eliminated seats and seats that did not take the last action, per [gameplay](../../05-gameplay.md). An official homogeneous Testing game is an integration check only.

**Two levels of evidence.** Use local simulation for rule checks, throughput and cost estimates, failure diagnosis, and candidate screening. Document any modeled speech responses and differences from the official runtime. Keep simulator and official results separate. Only fresh, official, mixed-policy Testing games support the tournament selection claim. If official mixed-policy play cannot be completed, report the strongest supported pilot finding and leave selection provisional.

**Fair comparison.** Evaluate one focal candidate seat against the same six frozen teammate/opponent policies; include Rules and varied LLM styles or frozen historical policies when available. Rotate roles and seats; pair assignments, seeds, and mixes where controllable. Record actual platform assignments and imbalances. Hold the LLM, action candidates, and other inputs fixed for component ablations where applicable. Use separate tuning and held-out mixes; freeze the run manifest, model and prompts, policy versions, budgets, and practical improvement threshold before final evaluation. Keep per-seat logs private and record game/seat/role IDs, configuration, legal choices, action and delivery status, decision latency, and authoritative result.

**Metrics and selection.** Report wins and evaluated seat-games, by role and standardized to the 4/7 villager, 1/7 seer, 2/7 wolf mix; show game-level uncertainty and paired differences where possible. Per [tournament scoring](../../04-tournaments.md), eliminated members of the winning side score a point, resigners score zero, and platform failures are unscored/replaced. Track no-shows, illegal actions, and timeouts with explicit denominators and separate agent and platform failures. If gains remain unclear, prefer more reliable action delivery and lower latency. A role-specific hybrid needs a fresh held-out mixed-policy batch and an official integration check.

**Gate for learned selection.** Proceed only if diagnosis finds recurring decisions with multiple distinct legal candidates, exploration is possible, and varied trajectories have trusted terminal team outcomes. Freeze the LLM and candidate generator; log candidate scores and selection probabilities, and compare a small learned selector to fixed selection and simple policy search, using terminal payoff. Promote a learned policy only after it clears the same practical gain, reliability, and official holdout gates as the other candidates. A simulator gain alone supports further testing.

**Evidence to retain.** Rule checks; terminal-log demonstration; opponent/seat/role/seed and budget manifest; separate simulator and official result tables with counts; failure analysis; selected frozen configuration or explicit unresolved gates. Keep raw observations and secrets out of public artifacts.
