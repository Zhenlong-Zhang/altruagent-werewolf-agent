# Semantic candidate PPO: implementation and prospective-test contract

Planner proposal, 2026-11-19. Parent owns acceptance and final deployment. This adds a trainable semantic policy while preserving the empirically stronger IDs incumbent. It does not establish a playing-strength improvement in advance.

**Data and isolation.** Finish the frozen E IDs-plus-seer-guard collection without editing its writer. Train on its predeclared 30 completed, eligible sampled focal-seat episodes, one fixed behavior checkpoint/generator/roster; archive rejected logs and reasons. Use only recorded before-action `day`, `phase`, eight candidate features, `candidate_labels`, candidate order, selected index, full behavior probabilities and acknowledged delivery. Coarse context is a deterministic serialization of day/phase, own-role bits, alive fraction and speech bit. No reconstructed future transcript, other-seat private knowledge or terminal-role input. Terminal faction score (-1/+1) supplies the training target only. New source snapshot/worktree and trainer cannot alter E. F remains unopened until training and artifact verification finish.

**Model.** Keep the legacy 20-feature design and collection weights as the initial logit prior. Frozen `sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2` at revision `1110a243fdf4706b3f48f1d95db1a4f5529b4d41` embeds candidate labels and coarse context into384 dimensions. Use eval mode, attention-mask mean pooling, L2 normalization and256-wordpiece truncation. A shared width32 attention block reads candidate features/embeddings and the context token, without positional embeddings or dropout; its shared scalar candidate head is zero initialized. Thus initial logits equal the behavior logits. A zero initialized critic reads a detached pooled representation; critic gradients cannot silently change the actor. Record heads, residual bounding, pooling and dtype in the artifact. Candidate permutation must merely permute probabilities. This semantic slate scorer cannot infer missing conversational history.

**Update.** One fixed-batch categorical PPO update, at most32 full-batch epochs. For each delivered sampled decision, freeze `A = terminal_score - V_old(state)` before optimization (`V_old=0` initially); keep these advantages fixed through all epochs, without advantage normalization this round. Minimize negative mean `min(r*A, clip(r,0.8,1.2)*A)`, with `r=exp(logp_new_selected-logp_logged_selected)`. Fit the critic separately by MSE to terminal scores. Adam learning rates: old20 coefficients0.01, residual actor3e-4, critic1e-3; gradient norm cap0.5. No undeclared entropy term, reward shaping or heldout-based epoch selection. Use the last accepted epoch. Single-candidate steps supply no actor learning; count and handle them explicitly. Log reduction/weighting, random seed and every accepted/rejected epoch.

**Trust and correctness checks.** Before each update, snapshot actor, critic and all optimizer states. Afterwards compute exact categorical `KL(old||new)` from the full logged slate, both mean across decisions and maximum per decision. If mean>0.02, max>0.1, or any value is nonfinite, restore the entire snapshot and stop; report zero accepted updates if the first step fails. These are empirical training-state constraints, not a guarantee for unseen states. Reproduce logged initial probabilities within existing legacy propensity tolerances (`rtol1e-7, atol1e-9`) using the legacy float64 prior path; selected log-probability, candidate dimensions and sum-to-one must agree. Zero residual is mathematical equivalence, not a claim of identical RNG trajectories across runtimes. Test clipping signs, terminal-target isolation, fixed advantages, encoder freeze, permutation equivariance and deliberate KL-violation rollback.

**Deployment and evaluation.** Encode locally on Hoffman2 compute allocations; cache embeddings by exact text plus encoder configuration. Measure actual cold/warm latency, peak memory and timeout/fallback behavior in the intended two-model mixed roster before gameplay. Encoder weights add roughly90MB in fp32 before framework overhead; measured memory controls allocation. Keep failures visible and use the frozen legal baseline fallback. F's primary causal contrast is old guarded sampled policy versus new semantic-PPO sampled policy, with identical candidate generator, inference dtype, state horizon, opponents, focal role/seat schedule and deadline. Preserve IDs greedy and guarded IDs greedy as incumbent controls. Freeze arm artifacts and fresh match/seed schedule before any F result; do not use F for training, hyperparameter choice or stopping an epoch. Report game counts, role counts, wins and game-level uncertainty, plus deadline/fallback counts. Thirty games remain30 terminal observations; extra decisions or32 epochs are not independent games. Training loss, factual-probe success or safe KL alone cannot establish stronger play.

**Primary-source alignment.** [Schulman et al., PPO, equation7 and algorithm1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.06347) support the clipped importance-ratio surrogate and repeated optimization of a collected batch. This contract uses Monte Carlo terminal advantages rather than GAE; exact KL rollback is an additional engineering constraint. [Vaswani et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) motivates the attention primitive; this small permutation-equivariant slate block is our adaptation, not their full Transformer architecture. The [MiniLM model card](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) specifies384-dimensional embeddings and pooling/normalization usage. Describe the result as a frozen-LLM proposer plus semantic PPO actor/critic: no language-model weight training, centralized critic, shared multi-agent policy training or full MAPPO claim.
