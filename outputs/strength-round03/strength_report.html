Round 03 · Completed bounded evaluation

Werewolf Agent: Strength Evaluation

Training and both prospective pilots are complete. The semantic PPO selector did not demonstrate improved playing strength. Retain the original greedy policy; winning the competition remains an unproven objective.

1. Objective and agent

Maximize faction wins in AltruAgent’s seven-player Werewolf game. A frozen Qwen3-8B proposer ranks offered actions and bounded speech templates. The learned selector combines eight numeric candidate features with frozen MiniLM embeddings of candidate text and the acting player’s coarse context.

A width-32 attention actor adds a bounded residual to the existing 20-coefficient policy. The actor and critic contain 31,766 trainable parameters. Qwen and MiniLM weights remain frozen: this is small-head reinforcement learning, not language-model token fine-tuning or full MAPPO. The optional seer guard excludes privately verified nonwolves only when an unknown voting target remains.

4. Completed greedy deployment pilot: Phase G

The same frozen checkpoints were evaluated on 54 fresh games, all resolved. G was declared before the aggregate F readout; an individual F canary had already been inspected for operational validation. The stopping rule required at least 12 completed games per arm, with a 75-game/three-hour cap. Oversampled games were retained. No parameters were changed.

The incumbent had the highest observed G win rate. These are small, role-imbalanced samples, so the comparison does not prove an intrinsic policy ranking. G recorded 239 acknowledged focal decisions, zero model/selector fallbacks or errors, and passed its configuration, controller and split audits. E, F and G match sets are disjoint.

What did the NN change? On the NN arm’s exact recorded guarded slates, its greedy choice differed from the old guarded head in 1 of 98 eligible decisions: one seer speech choice, in 1 of 21 games. One forced singleton was excluded. This is a comparison of recorded choices on identical observed states, not a simulation of alternative trajectories or counterfactual wins.

5. Delivery decision and limits

Retain checkpoint d7ebf7670444067a, greedy mode, no evidence guard. Keep the trained semantic checkpoint as an evaluated research artifact, without promoting it as a stronger competition policy.

The project delivered a reproducible trained semantic actor/critic and verified execution. It did not establish an improvement over the incumbent or tournament-wide superiority. F and G remain separate, roster-specific policy-selection pilots, not an untouched final test after selection. No adaptive tuning occurred within this frozen pilot; future improvement would require a fresh evaluation.
